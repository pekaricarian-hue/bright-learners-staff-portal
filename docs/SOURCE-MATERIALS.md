## Content authority

1. Current provincial legislation, regulation, licensing requirements, and official public-health guidance take priority.
2. Written directions from a licensing consultant or public-health authority apply to the identified centre.
3. Bright Learners orientation, handbooks, menus, and internal procedures supplement official requirements.
4. If an internal document conflicts with an official requirement, the official requirement controls and the conflict must be flagged for administration.
5. Verbal guidance must be labelled as verbal, dated when possible, and kept separate from written legal requirements.

## Course architecture baseline

- Alberta: 8 detailed modules, estimated 90-120 learning slides plus knowledge checks.
- Saskatchewan: 8 detailed modules, estimated 85-110 learning slides plus knowledge checks.
- Every instructional slide must carry a visible source reference.
- Every quiz question must carry a source reference in the answer review and audit record.
- Required pass mark: 100% for each module.
- Final internal certificate only after all assigned modules are complete.
- Default renewal interval: 12 months.
